# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who get a link to Jake from LinkedIn, an intro email, a talk, or a search:
hiring managers and founders, people running AI programs inside companies, event
organizers, classmates and professors (via /uvu). Usually on a laptop between
meetings or a phone in the evening. They want to know in under a minute who Jake
is, what he's done, and whether the numbers are real.

## Product Purpose

A personal profile, not a sales funnel. Jake is Manager, Internal AI Projects at
Huckleberry AI and isn't taking consulting work. The site tells his story, shows
the work with the counting behind it, and makes it easy to say hello.

## Brand Personality

Plain-spoken, specific, competitive in a friendly way. First person. Sounds like
Jake talking, not like a marketing page. No hype words, no em dashes in new copy,
no "Not X. Y." constructions, no stacked triplets, no exclamation marks.

## Anti-references

Generic AI-consultant landing pages: gradient heroes, hero-metric tiles, icon card
grids, "unlock the power of AI" copy, tier tables. Anything that reads like it was
generated.

## Design Principles

- Show the counting. Every number links to how it was measured.
- A person, not a firm. Photo, signature, career ledger, real details.
- Quiet confidence. Let the work and the ledger do the talking.
- One brand system everywhere: Manuscript (see DESIGN.md).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on paper and surface. Every animation respects
prefers-reduced-motion (MotionConfig reducedMotion="user" plus useReducedMotion).
No auto-moving content.
