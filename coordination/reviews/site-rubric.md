# Rubric: jakeheaps.com redesign

Written before review from Jake's stated goals and CONTRACT.md. Not changed between passes.

1. **Brand.** Reads unmistakably as Jake Heaps' personal "Manuscript" brand (warm paper, ink, cedar green for links/CTAs, sienna for numerals, Newsreader serif display, General Sans body, IBM Plex Mono for small labels and numbers, JH masthead monogram), modernized. Nothing reads as a generic template or as a different company's brand.
2. **Voice.** Copy sounds like a real person (a young builder talking plainly to a peer), not AI-written: no em dashes, no hype words, no tidy triplets or "not X, but Y" reframes, concrete details, varied sentence length.
3. **Story and stats.** A visitor learns who Jake is (background, career path, life outside work) and the four headline figures (93% adoption, 26 production systems, $2.3M first-year value, 1.65x productivity) are prominent and explained, not just asserted.
4. **Work page.** Features Messaging Brain, the Apollo outbound engine, a media monitoring agent, and the Domopalooza scheduler first, then other projects, in a way that is easy to scan.
5. **Mobile.** Excellent at 360 to 390px wide: every page and contact reachable from the nav, tap targets about 44px, readable type, no horizontal scroll or overflowing headings, sensible stacking.
6. **Constraints.** Current employer (Huckleberry AI) appears as name and title only; the only contact route is LinkedIn (no email address, no form) on the public pages; no "not taking clients / consulting" messaging; no screenshots of a former employer's internal tools on the public pages.
7. **Craft and accessibility.** Clear hierarchy and rhythm, AA contrast, visible focus, motion that respects reduced-motion, no broken links or blank sections.
